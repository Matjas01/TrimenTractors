About Trimen Tractors

AS Trimen Tractors sells used construction machinery, agricultural machinery, trucks and work tools for excavators and loaders.

We are in Klīves, Mārupe municipality, west of Riga. Everything we have for sale is listed on this website with photos, specifications and prices with and without VAT.

Each listing names the person responsible for it. Call or email them directly; between us we speak Latvian, Russian, English and German.
