Description

Caterpillar H65s hydraulic hammer. Pin diameter 50 mm, stick width 260 mm & 210 mm, pin centre distance 340 mm. Stock number TT1944.

Operating weight with tool 390 kg. Energy class 1085 J. Carriers: 5 to 9 t mini excavators, Cat 216 to 299 skid steer and compact track loaders, Cat 415 to 444 backhoe loaders.
