About Trimen Tractors

A/S Trimen Tractors is a Latvian dealer of used construction and agricultural machinery, trucks and excavator work tools. Our yard is in Klīves, just outside Rīga.

Every listing shows real photos, specifications and a net and gross price, so you can make a decision quickly. Call or email the responsible sales manager directly — we answer in English, Latvian, Russian and German.

  • Net and gross (incl. 21% VAT) price on every listing
